Level 1: High-Level System Architecture & Multi-Stream ERP Settlement Pipeline
Authorized Actors: Super Admin / Data Executive, Finance Team, ZMCC Manager, PHE Operator, MOT, Admin Operator, QA Lab, Weighbridge, ProductionWorkspace: All System Workspaces (/mot, /phe, /zmcc, /department/*, /super-admin/*)
▼
LEVEL 0: MASTER GOVERNANCE
• Super Admin / Data Executive: Adds Lab Tests & configures SOP Rules (RELEASE vs MONITORING)• Finance Team: Creates/Activates ERP Codes (erp_code), assigns Milk Pricing Rule (@13% TS vs Gross Liters), and sets Date-Range Milk Rates (from_date to to_date)
▼
LEVEL 1: UPSTREAM MOT COLLECTION & SHOP ERP SETTLEMENT (STREAM 1)
1. ZMCC Manager / Super Admin registers MOT Vehicle & MOT Profile.2. PHE Operator / ZMCC Manager assigns & dispatches MOT Journey (MJ-YYYYMMDD-XXXX).3. MOT visits Village Shops and submits milk collection (Quantity, LR, Fat -> @13% TS Liters).
▼
STREAM 1 — IMMEDIATE SHOP ERP LEDGER CREDIT
Upon each MOT shop collection submission, system immediately credits ErpSettlementLedger against that Shop's erp_code (@13% TS Liters × Active Date Rate).
▼
LEVEL 2 TO 4: ZMCC GATE ENTRY, LAB TESTING & LOCAL SUPPLIER SETTLEMENT (STREAM 2)
1. MOT Vehicle or Local Supplier (with erp_code) arrives at ZMCC PHE Gate -> Token issued.2. ZMCC QA Lab tests milk against Active SOP Rules set by Super Admin / Data Executive.
▼
◆ DECISION: What is the ZMCC QA Lab Outcome?
✔ IF ACCEPTED (Pass or Manager Approval)
• Post receipt to Sole Active ZMCC Tank Stock.• STREAM 2 (If Local Supplier): Immediately credit ErpSettlementLedger against Local Supplier's erp_code (Accepted @13% TS Liters × Active Date Rate).• Proceed to ZMCC Gate Exit (Restores MOT Vehicle availability).
✖ IF REJECTED
• Quarantine / Return Rejected Milk.• Zero Tank Stock posted & Zero ERP Ledger posted.• Proceed to ZMCC Gate Exit.
▼
LEVEL 5 TO 7: BULK DISPATCH, ADMIN GATE ENTRY & PLANT QA LAB
1. Bulk Tanker Dispatched (Independent Measured Vehicle Quantity + Average of Portion Lab Tests LR/Fat -> Whole-Vehicle Canonical Formulas).2. Admin Operator (ADMIN_OPERATOR) records Plant Gate Entry & issues physical Plant Token.3. Plant QA Lab samples and tests each compartment against Active SOP Rules.
▼
◆ DECISION: Are at least 1 Portion(s) ACCEPTED at Plant QA?
✔ IF YES (At least 1 Portion ACCEPTED)
• Advance VehicleVisit to Weighbridge for 1st Weight (Gross Weight).
✖ IF NO (0 Portions Accepted / All Rejected)
• Entire Cargo Rejected!• Bypass Weighbridge Gross & Silo Unloading -> Route directly to Plant Admin Gate Exit.
▼
LEVEL 8: WEIGHBRIDGE 1ST WEIGHT (GROSS) & BUSINESS DATE CUTOFF
Weighbridge Operator records Loaded Vehicle First Weight (gross_weight_kg) and captures gross_timestamp (PKT).
▼
◆ AUTHORITATIVE BUSINESS DATE CUTOFF: Is 1st Weight Time (gross_timestamp) < 08:00:00 AM PKT?
✔ IF YES (Before 08:00 AM PKT)
• Lock Assigned Business Date (operational_date) = PREVIOUS Calendar Day (e.g. 22-Aug).
✖ IF NO (08:00 AM PKT or Later)
• Lock Assigned Business Date (operational_date) = CURRENT Calendar Day (e.g. 23-Aug).
▼
LEVEL 9 TO 11: SILO UNLOADING, 2ND WEIGHT (TARE) & CONDITIONAL SETTLEMENT
1. Production unloads accepted portions into Raw Milk Silo.2. Weighbridge records 2nd Weight (Tare) -> Net Milk Weight KG = First Weight - Second Weight.3. Dual Reconciliation Engine computes Received Gross Liters & Received @13% TS Liters.
▼
◆ DECISION: Is Procurement Source a Company ZMCC or Direct Contractor?
✔ IF DIRECT CONTRACTOR (Stream 3)
• Credit ErpSettlementLedger against Contractor's erp_code (Received @13% TS Liters from Net Weight KG × Active ERP Rate for the Business Date locked at 1st Weight).• Admin Operator collects token & records Plant Gate Exit.
✖ IF COMPANY ZMCC
• Internal Company Stock Transfer & Dual Variance Only (Zero Vendor Payment at Plant, since Shops & Local Suppliers were settled at ZMCC).• Admin Operator collects token & records Plant Gate Exit.

Level 5: Upstream Bulk Milk Dispatch (Independent Vehicle Quantity + Portion-Averaged Quality Formulas)

▼
1. ENTER INDEPENDENT WHOLE-VEHICLE DISPATCH QUANTITY
• Enter Whole-Vehicle Quantity Value (KG or LITER) with Basis = 'MEASURED' (from Tank Dipstick / Flowmeter).• Entered independently as a physical measurement (never auto-summed from portion quantities).
▼
2. ENTER PORTION QUANTITIES & PORTION LAB TESTS (COMPARTMENTS 1..N)
• Enter compartment breakdown quantities (Informational difference = Vehicle Qty - Portion Total).• Enter Portion Lab Tests per compartment: Measured LR, Fat, Temperature, Acidity, Clot-on-Boil.

◆ PORTION QUALITY AGGREGATION: How many Portions (Compartments) are on the Vehicle?
✔ IF 2 OR MORE PORTIONS (N >= 2)

✖ IF 1 SINGLE PORTION (N == 1)
• Whole-Vehicle LR = Portion 1 LR.• Whole-Vehicle Fat = Portion 1 Fat.
▼
3. COMPUTE WHOLE-VEHICLE CANONICAL FORMULAS & COMMIT DISPATCH
• Using Independent Vehicle Quantity + Portion-Averaged Vehicle LR & Fat:  - Vehicle Density = 1 + (Vehicle Average LR / 1000)  - Vehicle Gross Liters = (Vehicle KG / Vehicle Density) or Declared Liters  - Vehicle SNF = (Vehicle Average LR / 4) + (0.22 * Vehicle Average Fat) + 0.72  - Vehicle TS = Vehicle Average Fat + Vehicle SNF  - Vehicle @13% TS Liters = (Vehicle Gross Liters * Vehicle TS) / 13• Verify unique raw_milk_dispatch_note_number, create VehicleVisit ('DISPATCHED'), and if ZMCC source, post ZmccTankInventoryTransaction ('ISSUE').

Level 11: Plant Final Dual Reconciliation, Silo Inventory & Stream 3 Direct Contractor ERP Settlement
Authorized Actors: Automated Transactional Engine (reconciliationService, siloInventoryService, financeSettlementService)Workspace: Backend Transactional Engine
▼
START: PROCESS NET MILK WEIGHT KG FROM 2ND WEIGHT
Verify accepted portions map to a single destination Silo (if > 1 Silo, fail closed with MULTI_SILO_ALLOCATION_REQUIRED) and lock Silo row (SELECT FOR UPDATE).

COMPUTE PLANT PHYSICAL, COMMERCIAL & DUAL VARIANCE METRICS
1. Plant Composite LR & Fat = Arithmetic Average of Accepted Portions.2. Plant Density = 1 + (Plant Composite LR / 1000) -> Received Gross Liters = Net KG / Plant Density.3. Plant SNF & TS -> Received @13% TS Liters = (Received Gross Liters * Plant TS) / 13.4.
