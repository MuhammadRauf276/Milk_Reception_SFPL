# Operational Discussion & Workflows
1. **Parallel Lab Gateway**: The system will allow trucks to proceed to the Weighbridge for 1st Weight once rapid tests pass, keeping long test values (MBRT) as a mandatory blockade blocking the physical unloading hookup.
2. **Row-Level Boundary Restrictions**: Contractors and local branch managers can only view their matching zone metrics. They are blocked from tracking other networks.
3. **The 3-Tier Operational Access Matrix**:
   - **Operators**: Input fields for their assigned station only.
   - **Department Managers**: View specialized progress logs, filter regional data, and hold data-correction key overrides.
   - **Factory Head (Main Admin)**: Oversees cross-department timelines and retains global data rollback controls.
